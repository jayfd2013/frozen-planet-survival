Description

Our political operating system was invented before electricity.

One person, one vote, on everything, all at once. Allegiance to one of two or three monolithic parties. A whole new government shipped on CD once every five years — then we sit and wait for the update.

Software had the same problem: how do you change a huge system people depend on without breaking it? It found answers. This PR borrows three.

I won't pretend to know what works. I never got to run that experiment. What doesn't work, I ran to completion. You are not gambling by changing the system. You are gambling by keeping it.
