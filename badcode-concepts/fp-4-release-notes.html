Release notes

Everything after v1.0 is a proposal. Nobody has shipped it. Not even me.

v1.0 Initial release, c. 1787

  • Pick one of two or three parties. Inherit all their views, including the ones you don't hold.
  • Known issue: no tests. Failures stay installed until the next disc.

v2.0.0 Composable positions proposed

Your housing view no longer depends on your defence view. Build your position from small parts.

v2.0.1 Rolled back a policy that failed its test rollback

It was measured, it didn't work, it came out. Nobody resigned. Nobody pretended. The test did its job.

v2.0.2 Every law ships with a time-to-live proposed

Default TTL: 19 years. Spec by Thomas Jefferson, in a 1789 letter to James Madison: the earth belongs to the living. Colorado shipped the first US sunset law in 1976.

v2.0.3 A/B test: basic income ran in production

Finland, 2017–18: 2,000 unemployed people got €560 a month, no strings. Employment barely moved. Wellbeing improved. Not what anyone predicted — which is the point of testing.

Known issues

  • Who represents the person whose needs don't fit the instrument? Open.
  • Diplomacy and judgement can't be unit-tested. They stay human.
  • These notes are probably wrong. Post better ones.
