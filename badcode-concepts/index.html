Twelve ways in

First design concepts for two sections of badcode.tv. Six for Future Proof, six for StoryVerse. Each one is a complete page you can scroll and tap. None of them is final. Pick what you like and we'll merge the best bits.

Future Proof

Rerunning politics with lessons from software engineering. The bad branch is finished; the good one is unwritten.

  1. 1Pull Request #1A merge proposal against democracy, with a diff, failing checks and a merge button that needs your review.
  2. 2Incident Report 2034A blameless postmortem of humanity's governance outage, written by the machine that watched it happen.
  3. 3Compose Your PoliticsA toy. Pick a party and inherit eight views, or build your own position piece by piece.
  4. 4Democracy, Boxed EditionLegacy software on a disc, updated every five years by post, against release notes for a system that tests and rolls back.
  5. 5future-proof.shA terminal session you drive with taps. The test suite for policy is empty.
  6. 6Three Wrong AnswersPoster-sized cautionary tales, posted wrong on purpose, with a stamp to object.

StoryVerse

A science-fiction wager against the multiverse: one stage, undecided, and you are on it.

  1. 1The Coin That Won't LandOne spinning coin. In many-worlds it multiplies. In StoryVerse it lands, because you looked.
  2. 2The Set Gets BuiltFog everywhere, and a stage that appears wherever you put your finger.
  3. 3The Last of the MagiciansA history lesson from the archive: Newton, the word "scientist" in 1833, and what got filed as wealth.
  4. 4Many Worlds, One StageA branching tree that redraws itself as one plot line across a night sky.
  5. 5Nobody HomeThe confession, one line per screen, with a switch that flips every line to its refutation.
  6. 6The Price ListDrag the cost of material things toward zero and watch the board reprice an afternoon.

Built from the canon in badcodetv/core: future-proof.md and the storyverse folder, plus outside research into explorable explanations and real precedents.

Every page has a back button in the corner.
