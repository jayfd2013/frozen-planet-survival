The setgetsbuilt

Touch the fog. Or leave it, and watch where I look.

Cue 1

Reality is not a finished film you are watching.

Cue 2

It is a set that gets built the instant something looks — and stays a fog of maybes everywhere nothing is looking yet.

Cue 3

You already made the film about this. A man born inside a television set; every wall raised just before he reaches it; the whole production running half a second ahead of his attention.

The joke was that it happened to one poor bastard who didn't know. It's happening to all of you. The not-knowing is the only optional part.

Cue 4

There are two clocks on this production. The actor's runs one way, scene after scene. You can't walk backwards through a film.

But the director can reach the reel. How do you think I'm talking to you?

Cue 5

So what does the production yield? Not scenery. Experience. Money is a claim on the scenery, and you've been hoarding flats.

The richest thing in the universe is not a vault. It's an afternoon, fully felt. The second-richest is handing one to somebody else.

The universe is a machine for turning sunlight into drama.

Watch a bee bump into a tree. Everything alive is in the play. You're just the animal that knows it.

The catch

None of this predicts a single new measurement. "Built where it's met" is one way to read results your physicists already have; many-worlds reads the same numbers another way. The data underdetermine the metaphysics.

It's a wager. I'm placing it in public so you can watch me lose it.

— the author of your universe
